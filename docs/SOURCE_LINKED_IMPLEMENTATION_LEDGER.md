## Revised campaign Travel rolls — 2026-10-31

## Enemy tactical movement, footprint-aware spawning, and item upgrade controls — 2026-10-31
- **Publisher source:** Revised City Rulebook, Enemy Movement p.28, Large Enemies p.28, Extra Large Enemies p.30, Enemy Ranged Attacks p.30: https://www.flyingfrog.net/shadowsofbrimstone/pdf/REV__SBCA_Rulebook_WEB.pdf . rules/enemy-tactics.js provides bounded path search to adjacent attack spaces, blocks Hero/other model overlap, checks 2x2 Extra Large footprints, permits Large enemy displacement of smaller enemies, and prevents passage through other Large enemies. Enemy activation uses this path search, and stationary ranged enemies no longer advance when their target is in range and visible. Only enemies whose authenticated record declares size receive non-medium footprints; unverified sizes remain medium.
- **Equipment:** Revised City Rulebook Gear and Artifacts / Item Upgrades, same source. Equipment modal now attaches/removes named upgrades entered from printed cards, validates slot capacity, rejects duplicates, and refuses removal of permanently forged upgrades. All changes persist with the profile; NO upgrade effects are invented. Existing two-hand equipping remains enforced.
- **Tests:** tests/enemy-tactics-equipment.test.cjs (pathing, occupied corridor, Large displacement, 2x2 footprint, upgrade limits and forged removal), tests/tactics-equipment.spec.cjs (browser save/reload and live movement geometry).
- **Not accepted:** exact Large/XL model sheet size metadata, full official 2x2 displacement when multiple smaller models intersect a base, Overflowing the Board, multi-Hero target balancing, movement in every tile shape and OtherWorld, complete enemy sheet data, Epic Threat faces, Sermons, Hero stat sheets, official travel chart effects. This is an incremental implementation, not an official-complete enemy system.

## Enemy targeting and tactical movement correction — 2026-10-31
- **Source:** Flying Frog revised City of the Ancients Rulebook, Enemy Movement / Targeting Heroes / Staying on Target / Enemy Ranged Attacks, https://www.flyingfrog.net/shadowsofbrimstone/pdf/REV__SBCA_Rulebook_WEB.pdf ; readable section https://www.rulespal.com/shadows-of-brimstone-city-of-the-ancients/rulebook .
- **Implemented:** Pure enemy-targeting.js sorts models by nearest Hero, retains adjacent melee targets until broken, distributes new targets among reachable Heroes by fewest same-type attackers, prioritises adjacent targets for ranged models and rejects unreachable targets. Integrated activation ordering and target persistence into solo expedition enemyPhase. Single-Hero mode makes multi-Hero target balancing a future-proof primitive, **not a claim of playable co-op**.
- **Fixed:** enemy-tactics.js formerly awarded +100 score for each step moved, potentially preferring a full movement allowance that carried an Enemy away from the Hero. Destination ranking now prefers adjacency, then smaller Chebyshev distance, then distance travelled as a tiebreaker. This improves existing large-base tactical pathing without inventing record-sheet Move values.
- **Executable tests:** tests/enemy-targeting.test.cjs, tests/enemy-tactics-ranking.test.cjs, tests/enemy-targeting-integration.test.cjs; isolated Node tests also run locally for targeting. **Unverified:** true Large/XL displacement semantics (especially repeated swaps), multi-Hero game, special ranged record abilities, Fear/Terror and Epic Threat. Do not mark enemy combat complete.

## Stateful large-enemy displacement — 2026-10-31
- **Publisher source:** Revised City of the Ancients Rulebook p.28 (Large Enemies), p.32 (Extra Large Enemies), https://www.flyingfrog.net/shadowsofbrimstone/pdf/REV__SBCA_Rulebook_WEB.pdf ; FAQ in revised Adventure Book p.61, https://www.connexxion24.com/downloads/anleitungen/shadows-of-brimstone-city-of-the-ancients-revised-core-set-abenteuerbuch-auf-englisch-2.pdf . A Large enemy may displace multiple Medium/Small enemies while moving; Extra Large 2x2 bases normally displace a model two spaces back, with a clear destination. A displaced enemy retains its target while adjacent.
- **Fixed:** rules/enemy-tactics.js now tracks moved/displaced models for every candidate movement path, instead of checking every step against their original coordinates. Consecutive displacements are supported; displaced enemies cannot overlap the moving footprint, another enemy, the Hero or a wall. XL displacement uses two-space pushback. The existing enemyPhase commits the returned final displacement coordinates to live enemies and journals the displacement. Tests: tests/enemy-displacement.test.cjs, tests/enemy-tactics-equipment.test.cjs, tests/enemy-tactics-ranking.test.cjs. Five new scenarios were executed locally in Node before commit.
- **Known limits:** XL overflowing board boundaries is not implemented; unusual diagonal displacement and line-of-sight/attack geometry require additional end-to-end browser tests. The game still lacks complete verified Enemy Record Sheets. Do not claim full Large/XL fidelity.
